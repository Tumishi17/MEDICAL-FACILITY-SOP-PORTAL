Approval and review

Before this portal is used operationally, the facility should identify the document owner and approving authority for each SOP, record approval and effective dates, and set the next review date. Review a document earlier if a major incident, legal change, new system or organisational change makes it inaccurate.

Publication checklist

  • All SOP content reviewed by the responsible staff.
  • Contact details confirmed and tested.
  • Approval status and effective dates completed.
  • Confidential network and security details removed from any public-facing copy.
  • Navigation and links tested.
  • Authorised hosting location confirmed.
