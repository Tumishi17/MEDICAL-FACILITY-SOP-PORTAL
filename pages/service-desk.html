Service Desk Procedure

Owner: Service Desk Lead (proposed) · Version: Draft 0.1 · Approval status: Not yet approved

Purpose: Provide a consistent way to report, prioritise, resolve and document IT incidents and service requests.

Ticket-handling steps

  1. Receive the report through a confirmed support email, phone number or ticketing platform. A controlled register may be used temporarily if no ticketing tool exists.
  2. Record requester, department, contact method, building/floor, affected asset or service, time, symptoms and error messages.
  3. Classify the ticket: hardware, software, account access, printer, network, server, security or service request.
  4. Assess priority based on impact, urgency and number of users affected.
  5. Give the user a ticket reference and communicate the next update where possible.
  6. Perform approved initial checks and record findings.
  7. Resolve within the technician's authority or escalate to the relevant network, systems, application or security specialist.
  8. Keep the user informed about progress, delays and approved workarounds.
  9. Test the solution and, where possible, ask the user to confirm that service is restored.
  10. Document the action, known cause, approvals, technician and closure date.
  11. Review recurring issues and report trends to the Service Desk Lead.

Suggested priorities

PriorityExampleAction
P1 — CriticalResearch management server unavailable; widespread network failure; suspected major cyber incident.Immediate escalation to responsible specialist and IT Manager.
P2 — HighOne laboratory floor disconnected or several users cannot access an essential application.Prompt assignment and frequent updates.
P3 — NormalOne user's computer or application has a problem but a workaround exists.Handle in the normal queue and track progress.
P4 — LowNon-urgent configuration or routine software request.Schedule according to workload and business priority.

These are proposed categories, not guaranteed response or resolution times. Management should approve service targets after reviewing staffing and operating hours.

Security escalation

Suspected phishing, malware, unauthorised access or possible information exposure must be escalated through the security incident process. Preserve relevant evidence and follow authorised containment instructions.

Document control: Proposed owner: Service Desk Lead · Review cycle: annually · Effective date: to be completed after approval.
