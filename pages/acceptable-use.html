Acceptable Use Policy

Owner: IT Manager (proposed) · Version: Draft 0.1 · Approval status: Not yet approved

Purpose: Ensure that facility IT resources are used responsibly, securely and for authorised activities.

Rules for all users

  • Use facility devices, email, network access, printers and research systems primarily for authorised work.
  • Access only the systems and information permitted by your role.
  • Protect credentials, lock unattended workstations and report suspected account compromise.
  • Do not install pirated, unlicensed or unapproved software.
  • Do not connect unauthorised routers, switches, wireless access points or other networking devices to facility ports.
  • Do not change IP settings, VLANs, routing, server settings or security controls unless authorised.
  • Handle research records and personal information only through approved storage and sharing methods.
  • Do not send confidential information to unauthorised recipients or copy it to unapproved personal devices.
  • Use printers for authorised work and promptly collect documents that may contain confidential information.
  • Do not attempt to bypass access controls, access another user's files without permission, scan systems without approval or disrupt services.
  • Do not use facility systems for unlawful activity, harassment, malware distribution or other prohibited conduct.
  • Use internet bandwidth, storage and printing responsibly so that essential facility work is not disrupted.
  • Report suspicious messages, unexpected system behaviour, lost equipment and accidental disclosure of information.

Monitoring and consequences

Use of facility systems may be logged or monitored for legitimate security, support and compliance purposes, subject to applicable law and approved policy. Breaches may lead to investigation, access restrictions or disciplinary action under the facility's procedures.

User acknowledgement

Before receiving access, users should be given this policy and a way to acknowledge that they have read and understood it. The facility should retain acknowledgements according to its approved recordkeeping process.

Document control: Proposed owner: IT Manager · Review cycle: annually · Effective date: to be completed after approval.
