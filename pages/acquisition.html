IT Acquisition Procedure

Owner: IT Manager / Procurement (proposed) · Version: Draft 0.1 · Approval status: Not yet approved

Purpose: Ensure IT purchases meet a documented need, are compatible with the facility's environment and receive the required financial approvals.

Procedure

  1. Identify the need. The employee or department records the business reason, quantity, location, urgency and estimated cost.
  2. Submit a request. Use the facility's approved acquisition form or process.
  3. Check technical suitability. An appropriate IT specialist checks compatibility, capacity, security, support and integration requirements.
  4. Check existing assets. Confirm that suitable equipment or licences are not already available.
  5. Obtain quotations. Procurement follows the facility's actual quotation and supplier-selection rules.
  6. Evaluate options. Compare total cost, specifications, warranty, licensing, delivery and support.
  7. Obtain approvals. The IT Manager records technical approval; the authorised budget holder approves expenditure under delegated limits.
  8. Place the order. Procurement orders only after the required approvals are recorded.
  9. Inspect delivery. Confirm quantity, condition and match to the order.
  10. Configure and test. Apply approved updates and security settings before deployment.
  11. Record the asset. Record serial number, asset tag, location, assigned user or department, warranty and purchase details. Record licence renewals where relevant.
  12. Verify the invoice. Authorised staff check the order, delivery and invoice before payment.
  13. Review and retain records. Keep approvals, quotations, order and asset records according to organisational rules.

Special considerations

  • Switches must support required port capacity and managed-network features.
  • Fibre equipment must match the installed fibre and transceiver requirements.
  • Server and application purchases must be assessed against service availability and recovery requirements.
  • Emergency purchases must use an authorised emergency approval process and be documented.
  • Retired equipment must be removed from the asset register and data securely erased where applicable.
Approval note: Do not invent quotation thresholds or approval limits. Insert the facility's authorised financial thresholds before this procedure is adopted.
Document control: Proposed owner: IT Manager / Procurement · Review cycle: annually · Effective date: to be completed after approval.
